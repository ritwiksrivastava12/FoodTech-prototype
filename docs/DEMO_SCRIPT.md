# FoodMate demo script (5 minutes, end-to-end USP)

Login: **demo@foodmate.app / demo1234** (Aarav — tired student, ₹100 budget, high-protein goal, paneer at lunch today).

## 1. Decide (60s)
Home greets “Good evening, Aarav”. Mood is pre-set to **tired**.
Type (or 🎤 speak):
> “tired, spicy, high-protein under Rs 100, 20 min — I have rice, eggs and onions, no paneer today”

Point out: **Egg Fried Rice** on top with reasons —
matches mood · fits ₹100 · 15 min · uses what you have · high protein · no paneer repeat.
Tap 🔁 Replace / 🔀 Regenerate to show non-repetitive alternatives.

## 2. Check (45s)
Open Egg Fried Rice → **Can I Make This?** table:
cooked rice 300g ✅, eggs ✅, onion ✅ (pcs→g aware), spring onion ✅, oil/soya ✅.
Note quantities, not just names. Switch servings 2 → 5: paneer-style scaling updates everything.

## 3. Compare (45s)
**Cook vs Order**: Cook ₹65 / 15 min vs Order ₹149–189 (demo).
“Saves ~₹100. FoodMate informs — you decide.”
**Shop** tab shows demo grocery/delivery comparisons, clearly labelled demo.

## 4. Cook (60s)
**Cook now** → Cook Mode: Step 1/5… timers (90s egg scramble), exact quantities
(never “some oil”), progress bar. Finish → **Done — I cooked it**.

## 5. Track (45s)
Completion updates **History + Nutrition + Kitchen** (rice/eggs deducted, shown).
**Nutrition**: calories/protein vs 2200/90g goals. **Planner**: today’s dinner filled;
**Kitchen**: quantities reduced; **Shopping**: generate list adds only missing items.
**AI**: ask “no paneer today” or share a fridge photo — uncertainty is explicit.

## One-liner
“FoodMate isn’t a recipe catalogue — it’s the decision layer between your mood,
your kitchen, your money and your goals.”
